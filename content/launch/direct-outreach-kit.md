# Direct outreach kit — the first 10 buyers

CLEAR's launch doesn't need an audience, it needs ten conversations. This kit is for reaching
people Erik already knows — consultants, HR / L&D leaders, product leaders — and asking them to
either be a Founding Partner (§ founding-customer-offer.md) or point him to someone who should be.

**Two rules that make this work:**

1. **Ask for a decision, not a favor-read.** "Would you take a look and tell me what you think?"
   gets a polite skim and nothing else. "Is this you, or do you know who it is?" gets a real
   answer. Every template below asks for a specific action.
2. **Personalize the first two lines, always.** Mass-send is obvious and it kills the warmth
   that's the only advantage this list has. The templates leave explicit `[slots]` for that.

Target ~25 sends to land 5 Founding Partners plus a handful of referrals. Track everything in
the pipeline table at the bottom.

---

## 1. Warm-network email (primary)

> Send individually. Personalize the opener and the "why you" line. Keep it short — a wall of
> text reads as a pitch, not a note from Erik.

**Subject:** A quick launch — and a question for you

Hi [First name],

[PERSONAL OPENER — one true, specific line. If you worked together: reference the project. If it's
been a while: "It's been too long since [context]." If you follow their work: name the thing you
saw them do recently.]

Quick reason I'm writing. I've spent years as a psychologist helping organizations move
behavior — getting people to actually adopt a change, not just understand it. I've turned that
method into a product called CLEAR, and I'm launching it now at clear-framework.com.

For the launch I'm taking on five **Founding Partners**: you bring one behavior you want a
specific group to change, I run it through CLEAR myself and deliver a decision-ready report plus
a debrief call. €200, one-off, with a 14-day money-back happiness guarantee. In return I ask for
a testimonial and permission to write up the case.

[WHY YOU — one line. Either: "I thought of you because [their behavior challenge / their role]."
Or, for a referrer: "You're not the target here, but you know everyone who is."]

Two ways you could help, if you're up for it:

1. If a behavior-change challenge of your own comes to mind, reply and I'll send the details.
2. If not, is there one person in your network I should talk to? A warm intro would mean a lot.

Either way, genuinely good to be back in touch.

Erik

## 3. Follow-up sequence (2 touches, spaced, graceful)

Silence usually means "busy," not "no." Two follow-ups, then stop — a third is pressure, and this
list is too valuable to burn.

### Touch 2 — ~5 days after the first email

**Subject:** Re: A quick launch — and a question for you

Hi [First name], bumping this once in case it slipped past. No pressure at all.

Short version: I'm running five Founding Partner reports for CLEAR (€200, money-back guarantee) and
looking for either the right challenge or the right introduction. If neither fits right now, that's
completely fine — just reply "not now" and I'll leave it.

Erik

### Touch 3 — ~7 days after touch 2 (final, graceful close)

**Subject:** Re: A quick launch — and a question for you

Hi [First name], last note on this, I promise. I'll assume the timing isn't right and won't keep
nudging.

If a behavior that won't move crosses your desk down the line, you know where I am. And if you're
ever curious, there's a full sample report here with no signup: [ERIK: insert sample link —
https://clear-framework.com/product/sample]

Good to be in touch regardless.

Erik

## 5. Pipeline tracker

Copy this into a Google Sheet or Notion table. One row per prospect. Update **Status** and
**Next action** after every touch — the two columns that keep this from stalling.

| Prospect | Segment | Source | Touch 1 | Touch 2 | Touch 3 | Status | Next action |
|----------|---------|--------|---------|---------|---------|--------|-------------|
| [Name] | Consultant / HR-L&D / Product / Other | Email / LinkedIn / Intro from ___ | [date] | [date] | [date] | New → Sent → Replied → Call booked → Partner / Referred / Declined / No-response | [what + when] |
| | | | | | | | |
| | | | | | | | |

**Status legend:**
- **New** — on the list, not yet contacted.
- **Sent** — first touch out.
- **Replied** — any human reply (route to a call or a referral ask).
- **Call booked** — intake call scheduled.
- **Partner** — paid, in fulfillment (move to the founding-partner tracker).
- **Referred** — gave an introduction (log the new prospect as its own row, Source = "Intro from
  [them]").
- **Declined / No-response** — closed. After touch 3, no-response becomes closed; leave the door
  open, stop the sequence.

**Weekly discipline (10 minutes):** count how many are still at **Sent** with no touch 2 due, and
how many **Replied** without a next action. Those two gaps are where deals die. Clear them first.
